The 4 Coffman Deadlock Conditions (1971)

Deadlock occurs if and only if all four conditions hold simultaneously in a concurrent multi-threaded system:

Condition 1

Mutual Exclusion

Resources cannot be shared simultaneously. Only one thread can hold a fork at any instant.

Implementation: Wrapped in fair ReentrantLock.
Condition 2

Hold and Wait

A thread holding allocated resources can request additional resources held by other threads.

Broken by: Central Waiter (all-or-nothing allocation).
Condition 3

No Preemption

Resources cannot be forcibly confiscated from a thread; they must be released voluntarily.

Broken by: Recover preemption action.
Condition 4

Circular Wait

A closed chain of threads exists such that P0 waits for P1, P1 waits for P2, and Pn waits for P0.

Broken by: Resource Ordering & Asymmetric Parity.

1. Naive Strategy None Deadlocks Guaranteed Left fork then right fork with 100ms pause. Satisfies all 4 Coffman conditions.
2. Max-Four Strategy Hold & Wait / Pigeonhole Deadlock Immune Semaphore(N-1) limits table occupancy so at least one diner gets both forks.
3. Resource Ordering Circular Wait Deadlock Immune Havender's principle: strictly acquires min(left, right) then max(left, right).
4. Asymmetric Strategy Circular Wait Deadlock Immune Odd philosophers pick right first; even philosophers pick left first. Breaks symmetry.
5. Central Waiter Hold & Wait Deadlock Immune Central monitor grants both forks atomically or none. No holding while waiting.
